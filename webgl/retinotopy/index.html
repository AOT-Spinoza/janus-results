Retinotopy at two voxel sizes — 3D brain viewers

Every retinotopic estimate for each AOT participant, at the two voxel sizes the data were processed at (2.0 mm and 1.25 mm), to choose which one to draw visual-area borders on. Each viewer holds exactly the layers of that participant's ROI-drawing overlay file, in the same order and under the same names. Both voxel sizes are resamplings of the same scans, acquired at 1.7 mm: 2.0 mm is slightly coarser than the acquisition, 1.25 mm a finer, smoother rendering of it (no detail beyond 1.7 mm).

Measured population receptive fields (pRF) come from the bar-sweep localizer, fitted with three models; every fitted parameter is included, plus each model's cross-validated fit. A colour fades out as the model fit drops: no colour at in-sample R² 0.2 or below, full colour from 0.6.

Motion-energy maps come from a model that predicts each voxel's response to the AOT movies from 2,530 motion-energy filters; a voxel's tuning is read off its filter weights. They fade over held-out R² 0.05-0.15. Its polar angle uses the pRF polar colours, so the two retinotopies compare colour for colour; its eccentricity is a centroid estimate pulled toward the frame centre and is on its own 0-2 deg scale.

At 1.25 mm the motion-energy model could only be fitted where the 1.25 mm main-task data exist: sub-002, sub-003 and sub-004 completely; sub-001 (10 of 12 sessions), sub-007 (9 of 12) and sub-005 (2 of 12) in part; sub-006 and sub-008 not at all.

In a viewer: choose a map in the dataset menu, drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and use the inflation slider to flatten the cortex. Needs a browser with WebGL; loading takes a few seconds.
