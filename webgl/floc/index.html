fLoc category localizer — 3D brain viewers

Category-selective cortex in each AOT participant, mapped with a video version of the fLoc localizer (Stigliani et al., 2015): 12 categories × 15 clips of 2.5 s, each clip shown twice, 7T fMRI. Each participant's viewer holds 15 maps: five stimulus domains, each in three comparisons.

Values are t-statistics from a GLM with one regressor per category, fit to each participant's 360 single-trial responses (348 degrees of freedom). Blue-to-red colour scale, saturating at |t| = 12 for the comparisons against baseline and |t| = 4 for the other two.

sub-003 also has the maps at 1.25 mm, from the same scans: the fLoc runs were acquired at 1.7 mm, so 1.25 mm is a finer, smoother rendering of that acquisition and 2.0 mm a slightly coarser one. The 1.25 mm analysis exists only where the 1.25 mm single-trial estimates do.

In a viewer: choose a map in the dataset menu, drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and use the inflation slider to flatten the cortex. Needs a browser with WebGL; loading takes a few seconds.
